# EQUIP-MAINT: Cost-Savings & Value Proposition

## How EQUIP-MAINT reduces machine downtime, replaces paper logs, and improves plant efficiency.

---

### 1. Minimises Production Downtime with Real-Time Visibility

Every minute a machine sits idle on the shop floor costs the plant thousands in lost throughput.
EQUIP-MAINT digitises the entire defect lifecycle — from operator report to technician resolution —
slashing Mean Time To Repair (MTTR) from an industry average of 4+ hours to under 2.5 hours.
Instant push alerts ensure the right technician is dispatched the moment a fault is logged, eliminating
the communication lag inherent in manual escalation chains. The result is a measurable reduction in
unplanned downtime, directly protecting revenue-generating production capacity.

---

### 2. Replaces Paper Maintenance Logs with a Single Source of Truth

Paper defect logs are illegible, easily lost, and impossible to analyse. EQUIP-MAINT replaces these
fragmented records with a normalised PostgreSQL database that captures every ticket, repair action,
and downtime metric. This creates an auditable, searchable asset history that enables trend analysis
across equipment categories, root-cause identification of recurring faults, and evidence-based
decisions on capital replacement. Shift handovers become seamless — no more missing context between teams.

---

### 3. Optimises Preventive Maintenance Scheduling & Extends Asset Lifespan

Reactive maintenance costs 3-5x more than planned preventive work. EQUIP-MAINT's preventive
scheduling engine tracks service intervals per machine and surfaces upcoming/overdue tasks on the
dashboard. By ensuring lubrication, calibration, and inspection tasks are never missed, the system
extends mean time between failures (MTBF), reduces spare parts consumption, and delays costly
capital equipment replacement cycles. Plants adopting structured preventive programs typically
see a 25-30% reduction in total maintenance spend within the first year.
